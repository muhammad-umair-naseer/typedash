How to play

  • Type what you see. A short paragraph appears and you copy it. Each letter you get right moves your car forward. Letters you get wrong turn red — fix them and carry on.
  • Getting words right in a row is worth more. The counter shows how many words you have typed with no mistakes. The longer that run, the more points each word is worth.
  • The boost bar fills as you go. Every word with no mistakes fills it a bit. When it is full your car speeds up for a few seconds and your points count double.
  • Three words in every paragraph are worth extra. They are highlighted. Type them with no mistakes and you get a bonus.
  • Typing on your own still counts. You race a robot that types at a set speed, or a replay of somebody else's run. It keeps your daily run going, but only races against real people give you points.
  • There is a new paragraph every day. Everyone gets the same one, and you get one proper go at it. Coming back day after day earns you more points.
  • You can play with friends. Make a team, and everything you all score counts together. Your country gets it too.
For the curious: how it works underneath
  • 10 Hz server snapshots stamped with server time, interpolated on the client — cars glide, they never jitter.
  • Server-authoritative progress. Validated and rate-limited; the race clock is synced NTP-style so everyone sees GO at the same instant.
  • Typing on your own never touches the server while you type — one result is sent at the end and checked against the typing-rate ceiling.
