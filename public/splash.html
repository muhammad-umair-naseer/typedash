How it works

Three things happen, in this order. There is nothing else to learn.

1

A paragraph appears

A few sentences, about forty words. Everyone in the race gets the same one at the same moment.

2

You type it

Every letter you get right moves your car forward. Letters you get wrong turn red, and you fix them and carry on.

3

First to the end wins

You watch everyone else move while you type, and the finish order is on screen a second after the last key.
